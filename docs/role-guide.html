1. The 8 roles at a glance

Every account has exactly one role. The role decides which kitchens you see and which buttons you get. Head-office roles see all 8 kitchens; a Location Manager and Kitchen Staff see only their own kitchen.

The Super Admin

The Super Admin is the single account that decides who gets into the system. It cannot be given from inside the app — no form offers it — and it is set only by IT on the server. If it is moved to someone else, the previous Super Admin automatically becomes an Execution Head. Nobody else can reset, edit or remove the Super Admin account.

How the kitchen score works

Each kitchen gets a score out of 100 every week: 40% cleaning (jobs done and approved), 35% expiry control, 25% labelling. A cleaning job only counts once a manager or head office has approved it.

2. What everyone can do

Sign in

  1. Open the app and type your User ID (usually your first name in lower case, e.g. rahul) and password.
  2. Tap Sign in. You stay signed in on that phone until you log out.

Signing in needs an internet connection. A wrong ID and a wrong password give the same message, on purpose.

First sign-in — choose your own password

New accounts start with the password 1234. The first time you sign in, the app stops at Choose your password and you cannot continue until you set your own.

  • At least 8 characters, and it cannot be 1234.
  • Keep it to yourself — everything you do is recorded under your name.

Change your password

  1. Profile → 🔒 Change my password
  2. Type your current password, then the new one twice, and tap Save new password.

Effect: every other phone where you were signed in is signed out. The phone you used stays signed in.

Forgot your password

  1. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?, type your User ID, tap Ask for a new password.
  2. The request goes to the Super Admin, who sets a temporary password and tells you what it is.
  3. Sign in with the temporary password; the app then makes you choose your own.

The screen says the same thing whether or not the ID exists, so it cannot be used to find out who has an account.

Create your own account (new starters)

  1. On the sign-in screen tap New here? Create your own account.
  2. Enter your name, choose a username and password, and pick your kitchen.
  3. Your account is waiting for approval. You cannot sign in until the Super Admin approves it and sets your role and kitchen. (Trying earlier shows "waiting for approval".)

Too many wrong passwords

After 8 wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes. The message says how long. To get in sooner, ask Husen, Manish or Rutvik to reset your password — a reset also unlocks the account.

Working offline

Once signed in, the app keeps working without internet. Cleaning jobs, photos and products are saved on the phone and upload automatically when the connection returns. A grey bar says "No internet — your work is being saved".

Notifications (the bell)

Everyone has the bell. It lists, for the kitchens you can see: overdue cleaning, missing photos, rejected jobs, jobs awaiting approval, expired and soon-to-expire products, and missing labels. "Mark all as read" only affects your own phone.

Log out

Profile → Log out. This also ends the session on the server, so the phone cannot be used to get back in without the password.

4. How work moves between accounts

All phones share one copy of the data through the Bookends server. What one person does appears on everyone else's phone who can see that kitchen.

A cleaning job, from start to score

1 · Job appearsCreated automatically for the week or month, assigned to the kitchen's manager. Status PENDING.
→
2 · Staff does itPhoto + Done — send for approval. Status AWAITING OK.
→
3 · Manager sees it"To approve" count, Home ☑ Approvals, and an "Awaiting approval" alert.
→
4a · ApproveStatus APPROVED, with who approved it and when. Cleaning % and the kitchen score go up for everyone.
4b · Reject (with reason)Status REJECTED. The kitchen gets a "Task rejected" alert with the reason; the job can be done and sent again.
  • If the due date passes while a job is still pending, it shows OVERDUE and raises an alert.
  • Re-open (↺) puts a finished job back to pending — use it if something was marked done by mistake. Only roles that can approve can re-open, and once a job is approved kitchen staff can no longer change it or its photo.

A product, from delivery to bin

1 · AddPhoto, name, batch, dates, storage place.
→
2 · LabelPrint, stick on, confirm "stuck it on". Until then: "Label missing" alert.
→
3 · Watch expiry7 days left → orange + alert. Past date → red + "Product expired".
→
4 · DiscardRemove it. It disappears from every phone.

5. Admin use cases

Step-by-step for the situations head office handles most. "Admin" here means Super Admin, Execution Head, Assistant Execution Head or Admin unless a step says otherwise.

A. A new staff member joins

  1. Profile → 👥 People, roles & passwords → ＋ Create a new user
  2. Type their name — the Login ID fills in (change it if you like: lower-case letters and numbers).
  3. Role: Kitchen Staff; kitchen: theirs. Leave the password blank for 1234. Tap Create user.
  4. Give them the Login ID and password shown on screen. On first sign-in they choose their own.

B. Someone created their own account (Super Admin)

  1. The menu badge / Home card shows "new account waiting for approval". Tap it.
  2. Check the name and the kitchen they said they work at.
  3. Pick their real role and kitchen, tap ✓. Or ✕ if you don't know them — the account is deleted.

C. Someone forgot their password

  1. Super Admin: Home card "password reset request" → 🔑 Set new password → type a temporary password → give them the username and password shown (shown once only).
  2. Other admins: People → 🔑 on their row → password becomes 1234.
  3. They sign in and must choose a new password straight away.

D. Someone is locked out ("Too many failed attempts")

Either wait 15 minutes, or reset their password as in C — a reset unlocks the account immediately.

E. Promote someone or change their role

  1. People → change the role dropdown on their row. Choosing a head-office role gives them all 8 kitchens.
  2. Ask them to close and reopen the app so it applies at once (otherwise within 15 minutes).

The Super Admin role cannot be given this way — ask IT.

F. A manager or staff member moves to another kitchen

There is no "change kitchen" option in the app today. Remove the account and create a new one at the new kitchen (their old records stay under their old account), or ask IT to change the kitchen on the server.

G. Someone leaves

  1. Super Admin: User Management → their row → ✕ Remove → "Yes, remove" → OK. Other admins: People → ✕.
  2. They cannot sign in again. Their past cleaning records and photos stay.

H. Weekly review

  1. Home → set the picker to All 8 locations → look at the league table and Surat vs Ahmedabad. Tap a kitchen for detail.
  2. Check ☑ Approvals and clear anything waiting.
  3. Reports → 🖨 Print / PDF to keep a copy, or ⤓ Export CSV to open in Excel.

I. An audit or inspection visit

Create an account with role Auditor for the inspector. They can see every kitchen, every job with its photo, and all reports, but cannot change anything. Remove the account when the audit is over.

6. The cleaning schedule

Every kitchen gets the same full deep-clean checklist. All kitchens are electric only — no gas appliances. You can view the whole list at Profile → 📋 Cleaning checklist (head office).

TypeJobsWhen
Weekly33Every week, spread across Tuesday to Sunday (each job has its own due day).
Every Tuesday1 of the 33Housekeeping area cleaning — always due on Tuesday. Shown in its own "Every Tuesday" section on the Cleaning tab and Home, with a yellow EVERY TUESDAY tag.
Monthly (heavy)13Once a month, due on different dates between the 6th and the 28th.

Areas: Floors · Walls & ceiling · Tables & surfaces · Fridges & freezers · Machines · Storage · Waste · Electrical safety · Staff area · Housekeeping.

New jobs appear automatically when the app is opened in a new week or month — nobody has to create them. Each job is assigned to the kitchen's Location Manager, but anyone at that kitchen can do it.

Cleaning tab tools: date range (This week, Last week, Last 7 days, or From / To), filter chips (All, Weekly, Monthly, Tuesday, Pending, To approve, Rejected, No photo, Done), and for staff "My jobs".
